# Evidence-based timer policy

## Product principle

Tomato Garden must not present one focus duration as a universal biological limit. It provides research-informed starting points and helps people adjust them using their own experience.

## What the evidence supports

- Sustained performance can decline over time, and brief, occasional task interruptions can reduce that decline.
- Micro-breaks of up to 10 minutes tend to improve vigor and reduce fatigue.
- A universal optimal break duration has not been established. Performance effects vary by task, and longer micro-breaks tended to perform better in the reviewed studies.

## What we must not claim

- “Humans can focus for only 25 minutes.”
- “Everyone must rest for exactly 5 minutes.”
- “This preset is medically optimal.”

## Product defaults

| Preset | Focus | Short break | Long break | Intended use |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Starter | 25 min | 5 min | 15 min | Balanced starting point |
| Deep work | 50 min | 10 min | 25 min | Immersive, demanding work |
| Light tasks | 15 min | 3 min | 15 min | Administration or low-energy starts |

These values are product heuristics, not experimentally proven optima. After each completed focus block, the app asks whether it felt too short, just right, or too long. After three ratings for the same preset, two matching “too short” or “too long” ratings produce a non-binding ±5 minute suggestion. The app never changes the duration automatically.
